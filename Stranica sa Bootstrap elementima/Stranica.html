TF2 Classes

Scout

The Scout is the fastest class in TF2. He uses speed and agility to flank enemies.

  • High speed
  • Double jump
  • Scattergun specialist

Soldier

A powerful damage class equipped with rocket launchers.

  • Rocket jumping
  • Strong damage
  • High skill roof

Pyro

Uses fire attacks and airblast mechanics to control fights.

  • Flamethrower
  • Airblast rockets
  • Spy checking

Demoman

Master of explosives and sticky traps.

  • Stickybomb launcher
  • High burst damage
  • Area denial

Heavy

The tank of TF2 with massive health and miniguns.

  • Highest HP
  • Strong defense
  • Minigun power

Engineer

Builds sentries, teleporters and dispensers.

  • Build structures
  • Support teammates
  • Defensive class

Medic

Heals teammates and activates the powerful ÜberCharge.

  • Healing beam
  • ÜberCharge
  • Essential support

Sniper

Eliminates enemies from long range with precision shots.

  • Headshots
  • Long-range combat
  • Pick key targets

Spy

Sneaky assassin who can use disguise and cloak.

  • Backstabs
  • Disguises
  • Destroy engineer nests

About Team Fortress 2

Team Fortress 2 is a legendary multiplayer shooter made by Valve.

The game features 9 unique classes, each with different abilities, weapons and playstyles. TF2 became famous for its humor, cartoon art style and competitive gameplay.
